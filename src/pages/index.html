Our Story

Our friend group consists of Abigail, Emmanuel, and Chrisley. While we were in school, a new student showed up in our class. He was depressed because he experienced many hardships in his life. But Abigail had a really good idea, making a game about his situation. After school, we started working on a game related to his situation. 2 weeks later, we presented him the game and asked him to play it. While he was playing, he started to feel like someone understood what he was going through and felt better. When he finished the game, he thanked us and said he felt a bit better. After he went home, we started making more games for people that had mental health problems.
